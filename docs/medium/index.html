How the score works — 60-90 DTE

Same scoring model as the short-term scanner, applied to medium-term contracts (60-90 DTE, ≤15% delta). Earnings risk is not excluded by default — at 60-90 DTE most underlyings will have an earnings event within the window, and the premium partly compensates for that risk. Annualized returns will be lower than short-dated plays; use total dollar premium per contract as the primary comparison metric.

Hard filters

  • Put options only, 45-90 calendar DTE
  • Absolute delta ≤ 25%
  • Bid > $0, open interest ≥ 100
  • Bid/ask spread ≤ 20% of the mid price
  • All of: bid, ask, last price, OI, volume, strike, expiry, IV must be present

Score components (max 100 pts)

Component Max pts What earns the points
Premium efficiency 25 Annualized return on capital secured. For 60-90 DTE expect 10-25% annualized; the 50% cap is the same as short-dated but fewer contracts will hit it.
Assignment cushion 25 Bigger discount from today’s stock price to the strike. 15% cushion = full points.
Delta safety 20 Lower delta earns more. Scaled to the 25% cap for this view.
Liquidity 15 OI (log-scaled) + spread tightness.
Earnings risk 15 Earnings before expiry = 0 pts but not filtered out. No earnings in 30 days = 15 pts.
Data via Yahoo Finance (yfinance). Delta and PoP computed via Black-Scholes. IV Rank derived from 252-day realized-vol range (HV20). Fair Value = average of Graham Number and P/E fair value (EPS × 15). Not investment advice — verify in your broker before trading.

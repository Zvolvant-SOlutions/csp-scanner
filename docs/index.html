How the score works

Every contract is screened through hard filters first, then the survivors are scored 0–100 across five components. Higher score = a better fit for a conservative cash-secured-put strategy.

Hard filters (excluded before scoring)

  • Put options only, expiring within 21 calendar days
  • Absolute delta ≤ 15%
  • Bid > $0, open interest ≥ 100
  • Bid/ask spread ≤ 20% of the mid price
  • Earnings before expiration (excluded by default; toggle in filters)
  • All of: bid, ask, last price, OI, volume, strike, expiry, IV must be present

Score components (max 100 pts)

Component Max pts What earns the points
Premium efficiency 25 Higher annualized return on the cash you set aside. Computed as premium ÷ strike × 365 ÷ DTE. Roughly 50% annualized earns full points; uses the conservative bid price by default.
Assignment cushion 25 Bigger discount from today’s stock price to the strike. (price − strike) ÷ price. 15% cushion = full points; smaller cushions scale linearly.
Delta safety 20 Lower delta (lower assignment probability) earns more. 0% delta → full 20 pts; the 15% cap → 0 pts.
Liquidity 15 Open interest (up to 10 pts, log-scaled: 100 OI = ~5 pts, 10,000 OI = 10 pts) plus tight bid/ask spread (up to 5 pts).
Earnings risk 15 No earnings within 30 days → full 15 pts. Earnings shortly after expiry → 8 pts. Earnings before expiry → 0 pts (and usually filtered out entirely). Unknown earnings date → mild 12-pt penalty.

Each ticker shows at most its top 3 contracts. The overall rank in column “#” is the global ordering by score across every accepted contract.

Data via Yahoo Finance (yfinance). Delta and PoP computed via Black-Scholes. IV Rank derived from 252-day realized-vol range (HV20). Fair Value = average of Graham Number (√(22.5 × EPS × BV/share)) and P/E fair value (EPS × 15); N/A for ETFs or companies with negative EPS / book value. Margin of Safety = (FMV − Price) / FMV.
